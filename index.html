🔔 Make Your Phone Ring When Nana Calls

Step 1 (Both iPhone & Android): Tap the blue "1-Tap Setup: Connect My Telegram Alerts" button on the main screen and tap Start inside Telegram.

Step 2 (Save the Ringtone MP3): In the Telegram bot chat, press and hold (iPhone) or tap the 3 dots (Android) on the ringtone audio file and select Save for Notifications.

Step 3 (iPhone Users): Tap Nanaphone_bot at the very top of Telegram → Mute (Bell icon) → Customize → Enable Notifications → set Sound to the saved ringtone. To make it ring even on Focus/Do Not Disturb, go to iPhone Settings → Focus → Do Not Disturb → Apps and add Telegram.

Step 4 (Android Users): Tap Nanaphone_bot at the top of Telegram → Notifications → Customize → set Sound to the saved ringtone, set Vibrate to Long, set Importance to Urgent / Pop on screen, and toggle Override Do Not Disturb ON.
