🔔 Make Nana's Calls Ring Loudly

Because Nana calls via Telegram notifications (6 rings over 50 seconds), follow the steps for your phone below so it rings like a real phone call:

Step 1: Link the Telegram Bot
Tap the blue "1. Connect Telegram" button on the main screen and tap Start at the bottom of Telegram.
Step 2: Set a Long Ringtone in Telegram (Crucial!)
1. Inside the NanaPhone Bot chat in Telegram, tap the Bot's name/photo at the very top.
2. Tap Mute (Bell Icon) → Customize.
3. Toggle Enable Notifications ON.
4. Tap Sound and pick a long melody instead of a short 1-second "Ding".
Step 3: Bypass iPhone "Focus / Do Not Disturb"
1. Open your iPhone Settings → Focus → Do Not Disturb.
2. Tap Apps → Add Apps → select Telegram and turn ON Time Sensitive Notifications.
Step 1: Link the Telegram Bot
Tap the blue "1. Connect Telegram" button on the main screen and tap Start at the bottom of Telegram.
Step 2: Set a Long Ringtone for Nana's Bot Only
1. Inside the NanaPhone Bot chat in Telegram, tap the Bot's name at the top.
2. Tap Notifications → Customize → toggle Enable Custom Notifications ON.
3. Set Priority to Urgent (Pop on screen).
Step 3: Override "Do Not Disturb"
Long-press the Telegram app icon → App Info → Notifications → select the custom category and toggle ON Override Do Not Disturb.
