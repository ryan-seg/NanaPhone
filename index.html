🔔 Make Nana's Calls Ring Loudly

Because Nana calls via Telegram notifications (6 rings over 50 seconds), follow the steps for your phone below so it rings like a real phone call:

Step 1: Link the Telegram Bot
Tap the blue "1-Tap Setup: Connect Telegram Alerts" button on the main screen and tap Start at the bottom of Telegram. (You should immediately get a "✅ Connected!" reply).
Step 2: Set a Long Ringtone in Telegram (Crucial!)
1. Inside the NanaPhone Bot chat in Telegram, tap the Bot's name/photo at the very top.
2. Tap Mute (Bell Icon) → Customize.
3. Toggle Enable Notifications ON.
4. Tap Sound and pick a long melody (like Opening, Radiate, or upload a custom 25-second ringtone MP3) instead of a short 1-second "Ding".
Step 3: Bypass iPhone "Focus / Do Not Disturb"
1. Open your iPhone Settings → Focus → Do Not Disturb (and Sleep).
2. Tap Apps → Add Apps → select Telegram.
3. Turn ON Time Sensitive Notifications so Nana's calls break through even when your iPhone is in Focus mode.
Step 4: Keep Banner Persistent on Screen
Open iPhone Settings → Notifications → Telegram → set Banner Style to Persistent so Nana's call banner stays on your screen until you tap it.

Step 2: Set a Long Ringtone for Nana's Bot Only
1. Inside the NanaPhone Bot chat in Telegram, tap the Bot's name at the top.
2. Tap Notifications → Customize → toggle Enable Custom Notifications ON.
3. Tap Sound and choose a long phone ringtone (rather than a short message beep).
4. Set Vibrate to Long and Priority / Importance to Urgent (Pop on screen).
Step 3: Override "Do Not Disturb" (Pixel / Samsung / OnePlus)
1. Long-press the Telegram app icon on your phone → tap App Info (i) → Notifications → Notification Categories.
2. Tap the custom category created for NanaPhone Bot.
3. Toggle ON Override Do Not Disturb so Nana's calls still ring even if your phone is on DND.
Step 4: Disable Battery Optimization for Instant Ringing
In Telegram App Info → App Battery Usage, select Unrestricted so Android never delays Nana's incoming call alerts when your screen is off.
